Your Device’s Afterlife

When a device stops working, its journey doesn’t end. It begins.

In India, electronic waste (e-waste) grows rapidly, with millions of tonnes generated each year. Your disposal choice determines whether it harms the environment and health, or contributes to safe resource recovery.

Key Insights

  • A single smartphone contains recoverable gold and copper worth up to ₹500.
  • Informal recycling recovers less material at higher human cost, especially in areas like Seelampur, Delhi.
  • Formal recycling reduces India's dependency on imported raw materials and supports a circular economy.
